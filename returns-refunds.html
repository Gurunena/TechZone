Returns & Refunds

Our policy for product returns, exchanges and refunds.

Last Updated: 2026
Legal Information
🚚 Shipping Policy ↩️ Returns & Refunds 🔒 Privacy Policy 📄 Terms & Conditions
Please note: Customers should check product condition before accepting delivery whenever possible

1. Return Eligibility

Products may be eligible for return if they are damaged, defective, incorrect or do not match the order received, subject to the applicable return conditions.

2. Return Period

Customers should contact TechZone as soon as possible after receiving a product when a return or replacement is required.

Return requests should include the order details and a clear description of the issue.

3. How to Request a Return

1
Contact Us

Contact TechZone through our customer support channel.

2
Provide Order Details

Provide your order number and product information.

3
Return the Product

Follow the return instructions provided by our support team.

4. Non-Returnable Products

  • Products damaged through customer misuse
  • Products without required accessories
  • Products with significant physical damage caused after delivery
  • Products that do not meet the applicable return conditions

5. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, please contact TechZone with your order details. Supporting photographs may be requested to assist with the assessment.

6. Refund Process

Once an approved return has been received and checked, the applicable refund process will be initiated.

7. Refund Time

Refund processing time may vary depending on the payment method and financial institution.

8. Exchange Policy

Where appropriate, TechZone may offer a replacement or exchange for an eligible defective or incorrect product.

9. Contact Us

For return or refund assistance, please contact our customer support team.
